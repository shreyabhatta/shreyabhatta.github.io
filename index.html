"I like making sense of messy problems."

I'm currently part of Zomato's Consumer Product team, where I work at the intersection of data, experimentation, and product strategy. I've owned analytics end-to-end for numerous features, from defining metrics and running A/B tests to building recommendation algorithms and shipping ML models to production.

My work spans the full consumer funnel: homepage discovery, search & autosuggest, restaurant catalogue, cart, live order tracking, post-delivery, and Zomato Gold. I've also worked on Flywheel (Zomato's off-platform Social Ads product), where I built audience cohorts and owned all data pipelines powering Meta & Google campaigns for 300+ brands.

I graduated with a B.Tech from IIT Kanpur. Outside of work, I read obsessively, hold a diploma in Indian Classical Vocal Music, paint, and am a serial coffee enthusiast.
